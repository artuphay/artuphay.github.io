Biller Integration Simulator

A working model of the host-to-host bill payment flow I manage with billers: inquiry, payment, advice and reversal, followed by the end-of-day reconciliation that catches disputes before they reach Finance. Pick a biller, enter a customer ID and watch every request and response.

All billers, customers and amounts here are fictional. No partner data is used.

Reference

Response codes used

00 Approved. Inquiry found a bill, or payment posted.
14 Customer ID not found at the biller.
88 Bill already paid for this period.
68 Timeout / pending. Send an advice to learn the final status.
94 Duplicate transaction ID.
05 Rejected, for example a reversal on a transaction the biller has not posted.

What this demonstrates

  1. Why inquiry comes before payment: the amount is locked by the biller, not typed by the teller.
  2. How a timeout is resolved safely with an advice instead of a second payment, which would charge the customer twice.
  3. When a reversal is the right call, and why the biller may reject it.
  4. The four exception types that cause most billing disputes, and who acts on each one.
