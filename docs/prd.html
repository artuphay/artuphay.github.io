1. Background and problem

PDAM Tirta Contoh serves about 180,000 household connections. Today customers pay at 6 PDAM cashier offices or through one bank. Queues peak between the 1st and 20th of each month, and about 12% of customers pay late, which costs PDAM collection effort and costs customers a late fee.

Our network has more than 4,000 counters and a mobile app in the same regency. Adding PDAM Tirta Contoh as a biller gives customers a closer place to pay and gives us fee income and foot traffic.

3. Scope

In scope

  • Postpaid water bill payment at counters (teller app) and in the mobile app.
  • Host-to-host integration: inquiry, payment, advice (status check) and same-day reversal.
  • Daily settlement file exchange and automated reconciliation.
  • Monthly fee invoicing and fund transfer to the biller's account.

Out of scope for this release

  • New connection fees and installation payments.
  • Partial payment of arrears; the customer pays all open periods or none.
  • Auto-debit or recurring payments.

4. User flow

Customer gives customer ID→Inquiry to biller→Show name, period, amount, fee→Customer confirms and pays→Payment to biller→Receipt with biller reference

If the payment response does not arrive within 30 seconds, the channel shows "processing", sends an advice, and prints the receipt only once the biller confirms. The teller can never resend the payment manually.

7. Settlement and reconciliation

  • The biller sends a settlement file by 02:00 for the previous day: transaction ID, amount, status, biller reference.
  • Automated matching on transaction ID, amount and status. Exceptions are routed by type: missing at biller, missing at our side, amount mismatch, status mismatch.
  • Funds are transferred to the biller at H+1 for matched transactions; disputed items are held until resolved.
  • Collection fee invoice is issued monthly, within 25 days of month end, based on matched transactions only.

8. Commercial and legal

  • Fee model: Rp2,500 paid by the customer, of which a share is passed to the biller as agreed in the cooperation agreement.
  • Cooperation agreement (PKS) and technical addendum signed before UAT starts.
  • SLA penalties apply to both sides for settlement delays beyond H+2.
